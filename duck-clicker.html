Operation: Duck Revival

A Global Wildlife Crisis

The year is 2026. For decades, unchecked hunting, habitat destruction, and pollution have pushed the world's duck population to the brink of extinction. By early March, scientists confirmed the unthinkable — fewer than 200 wild ducks remain on Earth.

On March 21st, the United Nations declared a global ecological emergency. World leaders signed the Quack Accords, pledging every available resource to one goal: bring the ducks back.

You have been chosen. As the newly appointed Chief Duck Officer, it falls to you to rebuild the duck population from scratch — one quack at a time. Start small, grow your flock, hatch eggs, recruit ducklings, and expand into a full duck empire.

The world is watching. Don't let the ducks down.
